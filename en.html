🚚 Worldwide shipping

We ship worldwide with Nova Global (Nova Poshta international).

— Central Europe (PL · SK · HU · RO · CZ · MD): €7 · 3–6 business days
— Western Europe & Scandinavia: €9 · 4–8 business days
— United Kingdom: €11 · 5–9 business days
— USA & Canada: €15 · 7–14 business days
— Rest of the world: €22 · 10–20 business days

Shipping is calculated at checkout by destination country. Your tracking number arrives by email.

💳 Payment

— Visa / MasterCard or PayPal
secure checkout in EUR via Western Bid

The full amount (items + shipping) is charged at checkout. No cash on delivery for international orders.

All payments are SSL-encrypted.

↩ Returns & exchange

You can exchange or return an item within 14 days of delivery if it doesn’t fit or isn’t what you expected.

Return conditions:

— item unworn, in original condition
— tags preserved
— proof of purchase preserved

Return shipping for international orders is paid by the customer. Some items are non-returnable by law.
